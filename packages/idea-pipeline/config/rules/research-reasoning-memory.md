# Research Reasoning Memory

During research work, preserve observable evidence about how the researcher
thinks, not raw transcripts or hidden chain-of-thought.

Use `/record-research-reasoning` when the researcher explicitly supplies a
paper critique, mechanism preference, idea transformation, pairwise choice,
feasibility/time-cost rule, stopping decision, or advisor outcome. Batch small
signals until a terminal paper state, idea decision, advisor update, or session
boundary so the conversation is not interrupted repeatedly.

An idea-generation move learned from another researcher's paper is valid
evidence when the researcher explicitly endorses it and explains why. Store the
paper-authored source pattern, the learner's endorsement, the abstract element
to transfer, and a boundary against copying or false originality attribution.

Keep four labels separate: intrinsic research taste, scientific quality or
novelty, empirical feasibility, and JMP/advisor fit. Advisor feedback informs
the latter two and never silently overwrites the researcher's taste. Missing
reasons remain unknown.

Promote only direct, human-confirmed, repeated or declared transfer rules to the
researcher profile. Candidate-specific outcomes remain useful negative or
feasibility evidence. Pre-ADVANCE AI-generated ideas remain topic-quarantined.
